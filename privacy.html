Privacy Policy for PromptArchive

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

PromptArchive converts and exports your AI conversations 100% locally inside your browser. Your chats, prompts, and exported notes never leave your device.

1. Zero Data Collection

  • We never collect, transmit, store, or sell your AI chat contents or browsing history.
  • Zero third-party analytics or tracking scripts.

2. Permissions

  • activeTab & scripting: Used strictly when you click the PromptArchive icon to parse the currently open conversation into Markdown/JSON/HTML locally.
  • storage: Stores your optional Pro license activation locally on your device.
  • https://api.gumroad.com/*: Contacted solely when you voluntarily enter a PromptArchive Pro license key and click Activate.
